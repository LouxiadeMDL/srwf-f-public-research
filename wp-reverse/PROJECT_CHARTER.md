# Project charter — SRWF_F_WP_REVERSE

## Canonical target
WP/YZZL modification of Saturn **F**.

## Non-goals
- no FF canonical merge;
- no execution of unknown historical binaries;
- no rewriting original ROM/BIN during research;
- no semantic promotion from guide/context alone;
- no cross-platform raw-offset inheritance.

## Evidence model
Every claim records a class and scope. A lower class may suggest a candidate, but only target-native evidence can close a Saturn WP physical/native mechanism.

## Definition of “彻底逆向”
The project is complete when the modification mechanisms are understood well enough to explain what WP changed, why it has the observed runtime effect, and how the same class of modification could be reproduced with auditable tooling.
